Common questions

A few things worth knowing before we talk.

Are the project prices fixed?

No. They are starting points designed to give you an idea of the typical entry level. The final price is based on the agreed scope, content, functionality and integrations.

What is not included in the €550 Business Website?

E-commerce, customer accounts, booking systems, complex databases, multilingual functionality, custom integrations, full branding, professional photography and substantial copywriting are not included as standard.

Do I need to know exactly what software I need?

No. Describing the business problem is enough. Part of the process is deciding what the simplest useful solution should actually be.

Is maintenance compulsory?

No. Maintenance is completely optional and can be discussed after launch.

Do unused maintenance hours carry over?

No. Included maintenance hours reset at the end of each billing month and do not roll over.

Does priority response mean the issue will be fixed immediately?

No. Response time refers to acknowledgement and initial communication. Resolution time depends on the nature and complexity of the issue.

What happens if I need more work than my maintenance package includes?

Additional work is discussed and quoted separately before it is carried out. Larger redesigns, new pages, new functionality, integrations and substantial refactoring are considered development work rather than routine maintenance.

Are hosting, domains and third-party services included?

Hosting, domains, paid plugins, external APIs and other third-party service fees are not included unless they are explicitly stated in the project agreement.

Can you maintain a website built by someone else?

Potentially, yes. Existing websites are reviewed first so I can understand their condition, technology and any existing issues before agreeing to a maintenance plan.

Can a project grow later?

Yes. Where practical, the first version is kept focused around the main business problem. Additional functionality can then be added when there is a genuine need for it.
