Business website case study

Flowers Bouquet By Anna

A bilingual website built for a handmade floral gift business that needed a clearer online presence, better product presentation, and a simple route from visitor interest to WhatsApp enquiry.

The project focused on a practical business outcome: make the offer easier to understand, show real work clearly, and give potential customers a direct way to start a custom order conversation.

Project snapshot

A small business website designed around enquiries, not e-commerce complexity.

This was the right-sized solution for a business selling custom handmade products where each order often needs a conversation about colours, occasion, budget, and inspiration.

Client type

Handmade floral gift business serving customers in the UK, including Romanian-speaking customers.

Main problem

The business needed a clearer online home outside social media to explain products and build trust.

Solution

A responsive bilingual website with product sections, gallery, order explanation, and WhatsApp calls to action.

Business value

A professional link the business can share with potential customers, social profiles, and local enquiries.

The problem

The business had beautiful handmade products, but needed a clearer way to present them online.

Flowers Bouquet By Anna creates soap flower bouquets, preserved flower arrangements, wedding and christening candles, corsages, car floral arrangements, and custom gift boxes.

Social media can show individual posts, but it does not always explain the full offer in a structured way. The business needed one clear website link where customers could understand what is available, see examples, and know how to make an enquiry.

The goal

Create a calm, professional website that helps visitors understand the offer quickly.

  • Present the business as trustworthy and professional
  • Show handmade floral products with real imagery
  • Support English and Romanian-speaking visitors
  • Explain custom orders without forcing an e-commerce flow
  • Make WhatsApp enquiry the clear next step
  • Work well on mobile for traffic coming from social media and messaging apps

The solution

A bilingual landing page with product clarity, trust-building sections, and direct enquiry buttons.

I built a lightweight static website with a clear visitor journey: understand the business, browse product categories, view real examples, learn how custom orders work, and contact Anna through WhatsApp.

The design stays soft and elegant to match the handmade floral niche, while the structure keeps the page practical: each section has a purpose and guides the visitor toward the next action.

Business value

What the business can do better with the website.

This is the part that matters for future clients: the website is not only a visual project. It gives the business a clearer sales and enquiry asset.

Clearer presentation

Visitors can understand the main product types without searching through scattered social posts.

Better enquiry path

WhatsApp buttons make the next step obvious for custom orders that need conversation.

Bilingual reach

English and Romanian content helps the business speak to both local and Romanian-speaking audiences.

Reusable business link

The website can be shared on social profiles, messages, local promotions, and customer conversations.
