The project

Beautiful products already existed. The online journey needed structure.

Flowers Bouquet By Anna creates handmade floral gifts including soap flower bouquets, preserved arrangements, wedding and christening candles, corsages, car floral arrangements and personalised gift boxes.

The business already had products worth showing. The challenge was giving potential customers one professional place where they could understand the offer, browse examples and know exactly how to make an enquiry.

01

The problem

Social posts did not provide one clear picture of the business.

Social media is useful for showing individual products, but visitors may still need to browse through many posts to understand what is available, how custom orders work and how to get in touch.

The business needed a permanent online destination that could be shared anywhere.

02

The goal

Make the offer understandable before the customer starts a conversation.

The website needed to present the business professionally while still keeping the buying journey personal.

  • Show the main product categories
  • Use real product photography
  • Support English and Romanian visitors
  • Explain personalised orders
  • Make contacting the business easy
03

The solution

A focused bilingual website built around product discovery and enquiry.

The final site guides visitors from the business introduction, through product categories and real examples, into an explanation of the ordering process.

Clear WhatsApp calls to action then turn interest into a direct conversation with the business.

A deliberate decision

Selling products did not automatically mean building e-commerce.

Many of the products are personalised. A customer may need to discuss colours, occasion, style, budget and availability before placing an order.

A full shopping cart and checkout system would have added complexity without necessarily improving that process.

Chosen approach Website discovery → product inspiration → WhatsApp conversation

The technology was therefore kept simple and built around the way the business actually handles custom orders.
