Process

A simple process for turning messy business problems into clear digital tools.

Good freelance development is not only about writing code. It is about understanding the workflow, choosing the right-sized solution, communicating clearly, and delivering work that makes sense after handover.

1. Understand the workflow

I clarify what needs to happen, what is currently manual or confusing, and what the finished system should help you do better.

2. Plan the right-sized solution

I choose a practical approach that fits the project size, budget, timeline, and future maintenance needs.

3. Build and test the core flow

I focus on working functionality, readable structure, responsive layouts, and testing the most important user journey.

4. Explain and hand over

I aim to leave you with organised files, a clear result, and useful notes so the project is easier to use after delivery.
